# Decision 001: Continue in functional medicine (rule in)

**Date:** 2026-11-16
**Status:** Active

## Context

The Sprint 4 goal was to rule functional medicine in or out. "Ruled in" was defined as one provider accepting the service at $99/month (or $10 per patient). Nobody has accepted. The Cerbo physician said he was "not against" the $99 pilot on 2026-11-02 and the Tebra physician leaned to a competing add-on on 2026-10-27, calling Reevive "definitely marketable to practices who don't want to switch."

## Options considered

1. **Rule functional medicine out and pivot to the marketing-agency lead-generation idea with the co-founder candidate:** the idea has an agency in the works, but it is still unvalidated and a 2-week trial with him is under way anyway.
2. **Rule functional medicine in and keep working toward a first customer:** a physician is still in conversation, and no evidence says physicians do not care.

## Decision

Rule in, keep working toward a first customer, with a changed approach: say plainly that this is new, offer to build it as a partnership with the physician, and set the expectation up front that it becomes paid after a short time. In Nick's words: "I did rule in functional medicine, at least that I will keep working towards a first customer." This does not meet the plan's own bar (one provider accepting), so it is a decision to continue.

## What would change our mind

Two design partners without start dates by the end of Sprint 5, or physicians repeatedly choosing built-in EHR add-ons over a partnership offer.
