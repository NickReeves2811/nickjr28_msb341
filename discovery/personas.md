# Personas

> this file should get *more specific* over the semester, not longer.

## Independent functional-medicine physician (the buyer)

- **Who:** Owner-physician at an independent clinic, 1 to 5 physicians, chronic-care patients, uses an EHR such as Cerbo, Tebra or Jane App and often one assistant.
- **The problem in their words:** the burden is lab and records follow-up more than charting. A lab imported weeks earlier can "fall off the radar." A prepped note with outdated labs costs about 30 minutes.
- **Current workaround:** assistants (15 to 20 hours a week at one clinic), templates, copy-paste, EHR add-ons and scribes.
- **What they'd pay / have paid for:** nobody has paid. $99/month offered to two physicians, one "not against it," one chose a competing add-on (about $375/month). One physician put a reminder-only tool at about $15 to 20/month.
- **Evidence:** [interviews 001 to 006](interviews/)

## Front-desk or clinic staff (influencer, not the buyer)

- **Who:** Receptionist or practice staff who feel the burden but do not decide.
- **The problem in their words:** nurses spend about 45 minutes a day on hold with a large lab.
- **Current workaround:** phone calls and faxes.
- **What they'd pay / have paid for:** unknown.
- **Evidence:** [interview 004](interviews/004-receptionist-2026-10-29.md)
